# Before you send the work to a client

The agent did these jobs in seconds. Checking took us longer, and it's the part the client pays for.

## Any job
- [ ] Read the agent's last message to the end. It often lists what it left out or wasn't sure about.
- [ ] Open the actual file you will send, not the agent's summary of it.
- [ ] Count: rows, products, pages. Same number as the client gave you?
- [ ] Pick 5 items at random and compare each one with the client's original, character by character.
- [ ] Anything the client didn't give you (a price, a weight, a review, a licence) is either removed or clearly marked for them to fill in.

## Website
- [ ] Open it on your phone. The first screen says what they do, where, and has a call button.
- [ ] Tap the call button and the email link. Right number? Right address?
- [ ] Every claim on the page (hours, prices, services, "since 2019") is in the client's notes. No reviews, stars, "24/7", "free estimates" or awards they didn't give you.
- [ ] No contact form that only says "Thanks!" without sending anywhere.

## Customer lists and other spreadsheets
- [ ] Open the result in a text editor, not only in Excel (Excel can hide a lost leading zero).
- [ ] ZIP codes, phone numbers and IDs keep their leading zeros.
- [ ] The duplicates it removed really were the same person (check 3).
- [ ] The originals are untouched.

## Product sheets and anything with prices
- [ ] Every price and weight matches the client's price list or supplier notes.
- [ ] Empty cells are listed for the client. Nothing was filled with an estimate, an average or a similar product's number.
- [ ] Nothing goes live at $0.00: rows without a price are drafts or left out.
